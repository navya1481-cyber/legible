How to pull these numbers from GA
1
Go to analytics.google.com → click Legible property → set date range (top right) to All time or a specific month.
2
Total Users & New Users: Left sidebar → Reports → Acquisition → Overview. Big numbers at top.
3
Virginia Users: Reports → Demographics → Overview → scroll to Regions table → click United States → find Virginia row.
4
Letters, Screener, Rights, Docs: Reports → Engagement → Events → scroll down to the table. Find each row by name: downloaded_letter, completed_screener, completed_rights_check, analyzed_document. The number in Event count column is your figure.
5
Log it here, then hit Save snapshot. Come back monthly to track growth over time.
